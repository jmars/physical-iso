# citations.md — the physical-iso paper's citation ledger

Rule (inherited from the isomorphism-paper lane): **no reference ships without a
row.** A row is created BEFORE the reference is used. Status is one of:

- `verified` — checked against an in-tree verified source (the dcare paper's
  References or the isomorphism-paper ledger);
- `verified (network: …)` — bibliographic metadata fetched from the named
  network source (INSPIRE / Crossref / arXiv) by a literature scout on
  2026-11-16, during the six-domain prior-work search (memos:
  `scouting/domain1-wheeler.md`, `litreview/domain2…6*.md`); the *claim* the
  row carries is the paper's reading unless the memo quotes it;
- `needs-human` — best offline knowledge; verify before submission;
- `program-artifact` — an artifact of this research program, titled from the tree.

Corrections from the scouting round are baked in: `Hog12` was the wrong paper
(the CQG 29, 195012 DOI does not resolve; the real one is PRD 85, 064007);
`NdV03` as titled does not exist (split into NgdV94/NgdV00/Ng03); `VPF13`'s
title and claim were wrong (its PRD 87, 122001 constrains Lorentz violation,
not voxel accumulation — the foam-blurring channel rows are Stein15/Stein23);
`Hol16` is now cited by its real first author (Chou16).
